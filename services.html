WHAT WE OFFER

Products & Services

From blasting solutions to environmental assessments, mine planning to equipment maintenance — we deliver end-to-end technical services for the mining industry.

Our Services

Eight core service areas designed to cover every stage of the mining lifecycle, from exploration through to closure and reclamation.

Mine Planning & Technical Solutions

Expert mine planning and design services including resource estimation, geotechnical studies, mine design, and technical report writing to JORC Code, NI 43-101, and S-K 1300 standards.

Blasting Solutions

Comprehensive blasting services including blast impact prediction modelling, blast monitoring and assessment, and blast optimisation for mining and quarry operations.

Environmental, Social, Structural & Atmospheric Impacts

Full-spectrum environmental and social impact assessments covering noise monitoring, pollutant monitoring (SO₂, NOx, PM₁, PM₂.₅, RESP, PM₁₀, TSP), structural assessment, and atmospheric impact prediction modelling.

Health Impact Assessment (HIA)

Comprehensive health impact assessments to evaluate and mitigate the potential health effects of mining and industrial projects on surrounding communities.

Mine Closure, Decommissioning & Reclamation (MCD3R)

Strategic mine closure and reclamation planning including decommissioning, rehabilitation, and remediation services to ensure environmental compliance and sustainable land use post-mining.

Technical & Standard Report Writing

Professional technical report writing services including JORC Code, NI 43-101, and S-K 1300 compliant reports for resource estimation, feasibility studies, and regulatory submissions.

Light & Heavy-Duty Equipment Repair & Maintenance

Professional repair and maintenance services for light and heavy-duty mining and construction equipment, ensuring optimal operational performance and minimal downtime.

Labour Supply for Plant Maintenance

Skilled labour supply services for plant maintenance operations, providing qualified personnel to support ongoing mining and processing facility maintenance requirements.
