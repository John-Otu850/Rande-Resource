Our Training Programs

Five expert-led programs covering the technical and regulatory knowledge critical to modern mining operations.

Explosives & Blasting Technology & Engineering

Comprehensive training on explosives technology, blasting engineering principles, blast design, safety protocols, and regulatory compliance for mining and quarry operations.

Explosives types and properties Blast design fundamentals Initiation systems and timing Safety and risk management Regulatory compliance

Mine Ventilation & Simulation

Training on mine ventilation system design, airflow simulation, contaminant control, and ventilation survey techniques for underground mining operations.

Ventilation system design Airflow modelling and simulation Contaminant control strategies Ventilation surveys Emergency response planning

Mine Dewatering & Simulation

Practical training on mine dewatering strategies, groundwater modelling, pump selection, and water management for open pit and underground mines.

Hydrogeological assessment Dewatering system design Groundwater modelling Pump selection and sizing Water management and treatment

Mine Planning & Design

Training on mine planning principles, design software, production scheduling, and economic evaluation for open pit and underground mining operations.

Mine design fundamentals Production scheduling Equipment selection Economic evaluation Design software applications

Mine Regulatory (JMO & SMO)

Training on mining regulatory frameworks, Junior Mining Operator (JMO) and Senior Mining Operator (SMO) certification requirements, and compliance with mining regulations.

Regulatory framework overview JMO certification requirements SMO certification requirements Compliance management Reporting obligations
